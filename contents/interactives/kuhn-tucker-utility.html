Kuhn–Tucker conditions for a Cobb–Douglas consumer

Maximize u(x₁, x₂) = x₁^α x₂^(1-α) subject to the budget constraint, then add an upper limit on how much of one good is available. At the optimum, the gradient of utility equals λ times the budget gradient plus μ times the availability gradient: ∇u = λ∇g + μ∇h.
